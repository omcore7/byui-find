ABOUT BYU-I FIND

Discover support you didn't know existed.

BYU-I Find is a student-created website designed to help BYU-Idaho students discover helpful campus resources.

Our student survey showed that many students know how to find resources once they hear about them, but don't always know what's available. That's why we help you explore resources based on your needs, not just their names.
