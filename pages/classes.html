10 resources
I'm struggling in a class Get help understanding assignments and difficult topics.

Try: Academic Success & Tutoring. BYU-Idaho offers academic tutoring to help students understand class material and work through questions.

How to get started

  1. Open the university's Academic Success & Tutoring page.
  2. Find the tutoring option for your subject.
  3. Check how to book an appointment or attend available tutoring.
Academic Success & Tutoring
I need help writing a paper Get feedback on essays, papers, and writing projects.

Try: Writing Center. Writing support can help you organize your ideas, improve a draft, and make your writing clearer.

How to get started

  1. Open the Writing Center page.
  2. Review appointment options and instructions.
  3. Bring your assignment instructions and any draft you have.
Writing Center
I'm having trouble with math Find extra help with math concepts and homework.

Try: Math Study Center. Math support can help you review concepts and work through questions from your courses.

How to get started

  1. Visit the Math Study Center page.
  2. Check current drop-in or appointment options.
  3. Bring the math problems or topics you want to understand.
Math Study Center
I'm nervous about a presentation Practice your speech and get feedback before class.

Try: Presentation Practice Center. Practice speaking and improve how you organize and deliver a presentation.

How to get started

  1. Open the Presentation Practice Center page.
  2. Check how to schedule a practice session.
  3. Bring your slides, outline, or speech.
Presentation Practice Center
I don't know which classes to take Get help planning your next semester.

Try: Academic Advising and I-Plan. These can help you understand course options and plan classes that fit your degree.

How to get started

  1. Review your academic plan in I-Plan.
  2. Write down questions about required classes or course order.
  3. Use the university's advising page to find help for your program.
Academic Advising
I don't understand my degree requirements See what you've completed and what you still need.

Try: Degree Audit and I-Plan. These tools help you check progress toward graduation and plan future classes.

How to get started

  1. Sign in to the BYU-Idaho student portal.
  2. Open Degree Audit to review your requirements.
  3. Use I-Plan to plan upcoming semesters, and ask an advisor if something looks wrong.
Degree Audit Instructions
I need accommodations for my classes Learn how to request academic accommodations.

Try: Accessibility Services. This office works with students who may need accommodations for a disability or other qualifying condition.

How to get started

  1. Read the university's accommodation request steps.
  2. Complete the application and any required documentation.
  3. Follow up with Accessibility Services about the next steps.
Accessibility Services
I have trouble staying organized Find support for studying and managing your time.

Try: Study Skills Center. Get ideas for managing your time, preparing for exams, and building study habits.

How to get started

  1. Open the Study Skills Center page.
  2. Review the available coaching or learning resources.
  3. Choose one study habit you want to improve first.
Study Skills Center
I need help with a specific subject Find available drop-in labs and subject support.

Try: Free Drop-in Labs. Some subjects offer extra help through drop-in labs. Available subjects and schedules may change.

How to get started

  1. Open the university's drop-in labs page.
  2. Check whether your subject has a lab this semester.
  3. Review the current location and hours before visiting.
Free Drop-in Labs
I need sources for a research project Find books, articles, and research help.

Try: David O. McKay Library. Use the library to search for books, articles, research databases, and other learning resources.

How to get started

  1. Open the library website.
  2. Search for your topic using the library's tools.
  3. Look for research help if you aren't sure which sources to use.
David O. McKay Library
