FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers

The questions students ask us most, answered in plain words. Tap a question to see the answer.

Questions

Getting started

I don't know where to start. Who can I ask?

Call or text the BYU-Idaho help line at (208) 496-1411 or email ask@byui.edu. They answer almost any question and can tell you which office to go to.

Is BYU-I Find an official BYU-Idaho website?

No. BYU-I Find is a student project made to help other students find campus resources. We link to official BYU-Idaho pages, so always check the official page for the latest hours and rules.

Money and food

How do I get a scholarship?

Many BYU-Idaho academic scholarships are automatic, so there is no application. They are based on your grades. Need-based scholarships come from your FAFSA, so fill it out every year (March 1 is the priority date).

See scholarship resources
Where can I get free food?

There are free food pantries in Rexburg, and many of the people they help are college students. You don't need to be in an emergency to go.

See food assistance

Jobs and career

How do I find a job on campus?

Every on-campus student job is posted on the On-Campus Job Board. Apply early, because many jobs fill in the first week of the semester.

See student employment
Where can I find a part-time job in Rexburg?

BYU-Idaho's Help Wanted Postings list part-time jobs from local businesses. For internships and career jobs, use Handshake. Never accept a job that asks you to send money or cash a check for someone.

See Help Wanted Postings

Classes

Where can I get free tutoring?

Tutoring is free. The Tutoring Center and Writing Center are on the 2nd floor of the McKay Library, and you book them through I-Plan. The Math Study Center (McKay Library 170) is drop-in, so you can just show up.

See tutoring
Who can help me plan my classes or change my major?

Academic Advising (Manwaring Center 102) helps you plan classes, choose or change a major, and stay on track to graduate. Book an appointment in I-Plan.

See academic advising

Health

Is counseling free?

Yes. Counseling is free for students taking at least 6 credits. Call the Counseling Center at (208) 496-9370. You don't have to be in a crisis to go.

See the Counseling Center
Do I need health insurance?

Yes, all students must have health insurance. If you already have your own, you need to submit a waiver by one week after the first day of class each semester, or you'll be put on the student plan.

See the Student Health Center
What do I do in an emergency?

Call 911 if someone is in danger. If you are in emotional crisis, call or text 988 (free, 24/7). BYU-I Security is available 24/7 at (208) 496-3000.

International students

Can I work as an international student?

Yes, on campus. You can work up to 20 hours a week during the semester and full-time during breaks. Off-campus work is not allowed unless International Services approves it first.

See work rules
What do I need before I travel outside the U.S.?

Get your I-20 signed for travel by International Services (Manwaring Center 276) before you leave, and carry your passport, visa, and I-20. Do it early, not the week of your flight.

See visa and immigration

Housing

Where am I allowed to live?

Single students ages 17 to 25 must live in BYU-Idaho Approved Housing (unless living at home with parents). Married students and single students 26 or older can live in any community housing.

Search approved housing
How do I get out of my housing contract?

You can sell your contract to another student on the BYU-Idaho Housing Board. Read your contract carefully before you sign, because it is a legal agreement.

See housing contracts

Campus life

How do I join a society or club?

BYU-Idaho has more than 140 societies, and you don't have to be in the major to join. Find one in the I-Belong app and click Join.

See student societies
How do I find out what's happening on campus?

Check the I-Belong app and the BYU-Idaho events calendar for activities, dances, outdoor trips, and service projects. Most are free.

See campus events
